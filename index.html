GhostleyTV Clipper

GhostleyTV Clipper is a personal, non-commercial tool run by the streamer GhostleyTV. It turns GhostleyTV's own Twitch stream recordings into short clips and highlight videos and uploads them, as private videos or drafts for review, to GhostleyTV's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. It has no other users and is not offered to the public. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.

Uses YouTube API Services

GhostleyTV Clipper uses YouTube API Services to upload GhostleyTV's own videos to his own YouTube channel as private videos for review.
