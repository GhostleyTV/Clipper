GhostleyTV Clipper - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

About the app

GhostleyTV Clipper ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial tool run by the streamer GhostleyTV (https://www.twitch.tv/ghostleytv), referred to below as "the owner". The app turns the owner's own Twitch stream recordings into short clips and highlight videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts as private videos or drafts for review. The app has no other users and is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

  • Platform access tokens. When the owner connects his own YouTube, TikTok or Instagram account, that platform issues an access token to the app. The app uses these tokens only to upload the owner's videos and to read the basic account name and ID needed to confirm which account is connected.
  • The owner's stream recordings. The app downloads the owner's own stream recordings from his public Twitch channel to make clips from them.

The app does not access, collect or store data about any other person, including viewers, followers or subscribers. It does not read comments, messages or analytics on any platform.

How data is stored

Access tokens and videos are stored only on the owner's private home server. They are never sold, never shared with third parties and never used for advertising. Downloaded stream recordings are deleted automatically after 14 days.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, the owner is also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

GhostleyTV Clipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to revoke access

  • Google / YouTube: visit https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove GhostleyTV Clipper.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, go to Settings > Security > Manage app permissions and remove GhostleyTV Clipper.
  • Instagram / Meta: go to Settings > Apps and websites and remove GhostleyTV Clipper.

Revoking access makes the stored token useless, and the owner then deletes it from the server.
